# CV backbone-only pretraining policy

Variant metadata lives under notebook.metadata.cv_baseline. Declare variant optimized/basic/hardcore and task_type classification/image_pair/detection/semantic_segmentation/instance_segmentation/keypoints/restoration/video_classification/action_recognition/tracking. Basic/hardcore require pretraining_policy="scratch", pretrained_components=[]. Hardcore declares training_status="untrained" plus its inference contract.

Optimized can use scratch or pretraining_policy="backbone_components". Only an image/video feature backbone checkpoint from torchvision or Hugging Face is eligible. A task head, detector, segmenter, pose estimator, restoration network or tracker must be newly implemented and initialized from scratch; never load a complete pretrained task model and strip/ignore its head to bypass the rule. ImageNet image-classification builders may supply visual features after replacing/removing their classifier with a new scratch head. For video, Kinetics classification backbones may supply clip features with a replaced scratch head. Do not use pretrained detector/segmenter/tracker weights or hosted inference/torch.hub.

Declare every component once with source (torchvision/huggingface), model_id, exact revision/weight enum, role (image_encoder/video_encoder), input, output and training (frozen/finetune). Backbone mode requires at least one component and an eligible visual task. Use concrete enums/revisions, not an unrecorded DEFAULT. A fixed image-only processor for the eligible backbone is allowed; fitted transforms use train only. Record actual freeze/fine-tune, BatchNorm/dropout handling and trainable heads in the proposal. Failed downloads must raise actionable errors; no silent random-weight or different-model fallback.

For Hugging Face choose encoder-only checkpoints that actually expose image/clip features. Do not use AutoModelForImageClassification, object-detection/segmentation heads or complete pretrained task pipelines. Model cards, checkpoint contents and real dataflow require manual review; static scans do not certify eligibility, licenses or contamination. Dataset/competition restrictions override these defaults.
